<section class="section" id="problem">
  <p class="section-label">The Problem</p>
  <h2 class="section-heading">Growth is when the shortcuts come due.</h2>
  <div class="section-body">
    <p>Every system is built with assumptions about scale. The platform that was right for one region and now serves twelve. The batch job that finished overnight until the data caught up with it. The integration between two systems that has since become the integration between nine. None of it was a mistake. It was the right call for the company you were.</p>
    <p>Then volume doubles, or an acquisition arrives, or a compliance requirement lands, and the architecture that absorbed the last five years cannot absorb the next one. The work is not rescuing a failed system. It is replacing one that succeeded past its design, while it stays in production and the business keeps running.</p>
  </div>
</section>
